## Getting Started

### Sign Up

BriefMe uses passwordless authentication. You can sign up with:

- **Google One-Tap** (recommended — one click)
- Apple Sign-In
- Microsoft SSO
- Passkeys (WebAuthn)
- Email magic link

### Onboarding

After signing up, a 4-step wizard helps you configure your morning briefing in under 90 seconds:

1. **Choose your modules** — Select which information you want in your morning digest
2. **Quick configuration** — Enter your address, connect calendars, pick news topics
3. **Choose delivery** — Web dashboard, email, push notifications, or all three
4. **Preview** — See your personalized briefing with real data

## Dashboard

Your dashboard shows all your enabled modules in a responsive grid layout. You can:

- Drag and drop modules to reorder them
- Click any module to expand details
- Access settings via the gear icon

---

## Modules

### Weather

Current conditions, feels-like temperature, and hourly forecast based on your address.

### Calendar

Merged view of your Google Calendar and Outlook events, color-coded by source.

### News Headlines

Curated headlines from your selected sources and categories.

### Health & Wellness

Sleep score, readiness, and activity data from Oura Ring and/or Apple Health.

### Daily Games

Quick links to NYT Wordle, Connections, Strands, Mini Crossword, and Spelling Bee.

### This Day in History

Notable events and birthdays from this date in history.

## Email Digest

If enabled, you'll receive a beautifully formatted email at your chosen wake time with all your modules summarized. The email supports dark mode and is optimized for mobile.

## Settings

Access settings from the dashboard to:

- Enable/disable modules
- Change delivery preferences and time
- Connect/disconnect calendar and health accounts
- Switch between light, dark, and system themes
- Manage your subscription

## FAQ

**Q: How do I change my delivery time?**
Settings > Delivery > adjust your wake time.

**Q: Can I use multiple calendars?**
Yes! Connect both Google Calendar and Outlook to see a merged view.

**Q: Is my health data secure?**
Yes. All OAuth tokens are encrypted at rest and health data is cached temporarily, never stored permanently.
